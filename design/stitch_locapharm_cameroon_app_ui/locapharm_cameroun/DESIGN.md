## Brand & Style

This design system powers a premium, ultra-precise pharmacy location service tailored for urban Cameroon (Douala, Yaoundé, Bafoussam, Garoua). The visual signature fuses hyper-refined European functionalism with an energetic African metropolis velocity: pure clarity, rapid orientation, and dependable medical reassurance.

The design movement combines **Modern High-Contrast Precision** with **Organic Tactility**:
- Crisp, clinical utility softened by deep botanical sage tones (#5B7A4E and #3F5A36), evoking natural health, authority, and tranquility in medical distress.
- Kinetic tactical pops of vibrant high-voltage lime (#C8F000) for wayfinding, routes, live markers, and user presence, cutting through visual noise under bright equatorial sunlight.
- Hyper-curved pill architecture with soft tactile depth and ambient daylight shadows, discarding harsh technological dividers in favor of generous, protective physical containers.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate hierarchy between clinical safety, urgent navigation, and structural contrast:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Application Canvas: `#E9EAEB` (Light technical grey that reduces glare and frames white containers).
  - Surface Containers: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for elevated cards, bottom sheets, and interactive elements).
  - Immersive Surfaces (Splash, Onboarding, Permission sheets): `#3F5A36` (Deep dark sage green).
- **Core Tones:**
  - **Primary (`#5B7A4E`):** Deep sage green conveying trust, health, and medical authority.
  - **Secondary / Accent (`#C8F000`):** Electric lime for routes, active map pins, live pulses, and progress tracks.
  - **Tertiary (`#3F5A36`):** Dark sage for high-emphasis callouts, "DE GARDE" nocturnal status, and immersive backgrounds.
- **Typography & Neutrals:**
  - Primary Text: `#12161A` (Near-black, high contrast).
  - Secondary Text: `#5E656B` (Mid-tone grey for metadata, opening hours, subheadings).
  - Muted / Disabled: `#9AA0A6` (Subtle boundary borders, icons, inactive indicators).
- **Status Badges:**
  - `DE GARDE`: Solid `#3F5A36` with `#FFFFFF` text.
  - `OUVERTE`: Solid `#0F1110` with `#C8F000` text.
  - `FERMÉE`: Solid `#E2E4E6` with `#5E656B` text.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs the structural, expansive energy of **Syne** with the clinical legibility of **Inter**:

- **Headlines (Syne):** Wide, assertive, geometric grotesque architecture. Gives emergency headers, pharmacy brand titles, and neighborhood identifiers ("AKWA", "BASTOS", "BONAPRISO") prominent character.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** Highly legible across varied mobile screen densities and sunlight conditions.
- **Data & Numbers:** All numeric parameters (distance in meters/km, travel time in minutes, phone contacts) must employ `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` or `font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1` within Inter to guarantee clear optical vertical alignment in comparison lists.
- **Capitalization:** All badge indicators (`DE GARDE`, `OUVERTE`, `FERMÉE`) use uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (+0.04em to +0.06em).

## Layout & Spacing

Designed specifically for handheld Android hardware with one-handed thumb navigation zones:

- **Grid & Margins:**
  - Mobile (Standard Android portrait 360dp - 412dp): 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) screen margins.
  - Large viewports / Foldables unfolded (600dp+): 8 columns with capped content width at 560dp for bottom sheets to preserve ergonomics.
- **Thumb Zone Principle:**
  - Interactive map triggers, floating pills, and actionable sheets anchor strictly to the bottom 60% of the viewport.
  - Search queries and status filters stay reachable via bottom-docked or expandable floating sheets.
- **Component Gaps & Padding:**
  - Use `space-xs` (4px) for badge inner vertical gaps and icon-to-text separation.
  - Use `space-sm` (8px) for list element separation and horizontal pill internal gaps.
  - Use `space-md` (16px) for card body internal padding and form field vertical flow.
  - Use `space-lg` (24px) for card container padding and primary modal step separation.
  - Use `space-xl` (32px) for sheet edge clearance and section separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses pure surface contrast and diffused ambient illumination. Harsh outlines and aggressive multi-drop shadows are avoided entirely.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):**
  - Surface: `#E9EAEB` (Light grey base canvas). No elevation.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Surface Tiles):**
  - Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white).
  - Ambient Shadow: `0px 4px 20px rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.04), 0px 1px 4px rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.02)`.
  - Border: None. Edge defined purely by luminance shift between `#FFFFFF` and `#E9EAEB`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Buttons & Map Controls):**
  - Surface: `#FFFFFF` 44px round icon buttons.
  - Ambient Shadow: `0px 8px 24px rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Bottom Sheets):**
  - Surface: `#FFFFFF` with top border radius `32px`.
  - Ambient Shadow: `0px -8px 32px rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.08)`.
  - Backdrop Scrim: `rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.35)` with a 4px blur fallback on supported Android views.
- **Level 4 (Floating Bottom Navigation Bar):**
  - Surface: `#0F1110` pill container floating 16px above navigation bar inset.
  - Ambient Shadow: `0px 12px 36px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The system relies on an exaggerated, ultra-protective pill geometry:

- **Buttons & Interactive Tags:** Absolute pill radius (`9999px`) across all sizes.
- **Cards & Pharmacy Modules:** `28px` corner radius. This curved container differentiates pharmacy cards from conventional utility cards.
- **Bottom Sheets (Interactive Drawer):** `32px` on top-left and top-right corners; `0px` on bottom corners.
- **Icon Floating Buttons:** Perfect circles (`50%` radius, fixed 44px × 44px).
- **Search & Input Bars:** Pill-shaped (`9999px` radius) with 56px touch target height.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Call / Route):**
  - Full-width pill (`9999px` radius), height 56px.
  - Background `#0F1110` (Near Black), text `#FFFFFF` in `label-lg` (Inter 14px Semibold).
  - Must include a trailing diagonal navigation arrow icon: `↗` (white, 18px).
- **Secondary Action (Guard Duty / Reserve):**
  - Full-width pill, height 56px.
  - Background `#5B7A4E` (Deep Sage), text `#FFFFFF` with trailing arrow `↗` or telephone glyph.
- **Round Icon Controls:**
  - Fixed 44px × 44px, circular `50%`.
  - Background `#FFFFFF`, primary icon `#12161A`, subtle ambient shadow.
  - Active/Pressed state triggers `#F0F2F1` surface tint.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Status Tags:** Compact pills with internal padding `4px 12px`.
  - `DE GARDE`: Background `#3F5A36`, Text `#FFFFFF`, `label-sm`.
  - `OUVERTE`: Background `#0F1110`, Text `#C8F000`, `label-sm`.
  - `FERMÉE`: Background `#E2E4E6`, Text `#5E656B`, `label-sm`.
- **Filter Chips:**
  - Height 36px, fully rounded `9999px`.
  - Inactive: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#5E656B`.
  - Active: Background `#0F1110`, text `#FFFFFF`, with a 6px dot in `#C8F000`.

### Cards (Pharmacy Result Card)
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF`, radius `28px`, padding 20px.
- **Header:** Syne headline-md (`#12161A`) alongside the relevant Status Badge (`OUVERTE` or `DE GARDE`).
- **Body Details:**
  - Distance & Travel Time rendered in `data-metric` with tabular figures (e.g., `850 m • 4 min`).
  - Neighborhood subtitle in `body-sm` (`#5E656B`) (e.g., `Akwa, face Direction Générale MTN`).
- **Footer Action:** Embedded primary black pill button or direct call button.

### Bottom Navigation Bar (Floating Pill)
- Docked floating pill suspended `16px` above the bottom screen safe area, centered with `24px` horizontal margins.
- Background `#0F1110`, padding `6px 8px`, fully rounded `9999px`.
- **Active Navigation Item:** Pure `#FFFFFF` circular pill wrapping the active icon in `#0F1110`.
- **Inactive Navigation Items:** Translucent `#9AA0A6` icon glyphs.

### Inputs & Search
- Height 56px, fully rounded `9999px`.
- Background `#FFFFFF`, ambient shadow Level 1.
- Leading search icon in `#12161A`, placeholder text in `#9AA0A6` ("Rechercher une pharmacie, un quartier...").
- Trailing quick-filter button or GPS trigger in `#C8F000`.

### Map Markers & Route Indicators
- **User Location Dot:** `#C8F000` solid dot (14px) with a translucent breathing pulse ring (`rgba(200, 240, 0, 0.3)`).
- **Pharmacy Pins:**
  - Standard: `#0F1110` pill marker displaying pharmacy opening status.
  - De Garde Night Pin: `#3F5A36` marker with white cross emblem.
  - Active Selected Pin: Scaled up with vibrant `#C8F000` rim.
- **Route Polyline:** `#C8F000` high-contrast path with `#0F1110` casing for maximum visibility across terrain and satellite tiles.
