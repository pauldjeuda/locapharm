opening_hours.js interprets schedules copied from OpenStreetMap. Its result is a
reading of the mapped opening_hours value, not live confirmation, a duty roster,
or a guarantee that a pharmacy is open. Locapharm keeps conditional or
uninterpretable values unknown and tells users to call before travelling.
