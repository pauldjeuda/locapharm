# Privacy principles

Locapharm has no account and no behavioral analytics. Nearby pharmacy search and schedule calculations use device-local data. The device's location is not uploaded for nearby search. A user-requested route may send approximate start/end points to a routing provider through the Worker; those coordinates are not retained or written to logs. Optional report contact details require explicit consent and are deleted after the documented retention period once that service exists.

Request location, microphone/speech, and notification permissions only after the user starts the related feature. Do not show permission prompts, play speech, vibrate, or schedule notifications during app startup.

This is a design statement for the planned architecture, not a claim about a deployed service. The final public privacy notice must be reviewed against implemented providers and applicable Cameroon requirements before release.
